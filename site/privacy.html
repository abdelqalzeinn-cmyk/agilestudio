Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-02

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum necessary to operate AgileStudio:

  • Account data: your email address, display name, and a hashed password (never stored in plaintext).
  • Roblox identity (optional): if you sign in with Roblox, we store your Roblox user ID and username as provided by Roblox's OAuth service. We do not receive your Roblox password.
  • Usage data: request counts, model used, and approximate cost, stored per account for quota and billing purposes.
  • API tokens: tokens are stored hashed; only the first characters are ever displayed back to you.
  • Chat content: messages you send may be forwarded to third-party language-model providers to generate a response. We do not permanently store chat message bodies beyond the current session.

2. How We Use Information

To authenticate you, enforce usage quotas, provide the chat/tooling features, and improve the Service. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-Party Providers

When you use chat features, your prompt and conversation context are sent to our model provider (FreeLLMAPI) for processing. If you sign in with Roblox, authentication is handled by Roblox's OAuth service under their privacy policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain account and usage records while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time via the project's GitHub issues page.

5. Security

Passwords are hashed with a salted algorithm. API tokens are hashed at rest. Sessions use HTTP-only cookies.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the repository owner via the project's GitHub issues page.
